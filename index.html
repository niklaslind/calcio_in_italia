BEFORE ANYONE BOOKS

Match first. Tickets next. Then the journey.

  1. Agree on a route and check a home fixture for the correct season. Keep a second local club in reserve.
  2. Confirm the opponent, stadium, day and kickoff with the club. Buy only through club-authorised ticket channels; check named-ticket and ID requirements.
  3. Search flights from Stockholm for your actual dates. A suggested airport does not imply a nonstop flight. Check baggage and airport transfer time.
  4. For four adults, request two twin rooms with four separate beds. Choose a walkable base near the station and allow a weatherproof day.
  5. Leave a buffer after the match and before the flight. Check winter ferry services, trail conditions and the mid-December rail timetable change.
